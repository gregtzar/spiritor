# Question or topic researched

## Question

What we needed to find out, and why. Name the decision or spec it informs.

## Recommendation

### Summary

What we recommend, in a short table or a few bullets, with the main reasons. A reader who stops here knows what we'd do. It's input to a decision, not the decision itself: the choice made is recorded in `docs/decisions/` or a spec, which links back here.

### Details

How the recommendation would be applied, and anything else needed to act on it.

## Findings

### Summary

A comparison table: one row per option, one column per criterion that matters for the question (for example what it covers, cost, limits), and a one-line verdict per option.

### Details

One subsection per option, holding only what doesn't fit in the table: how it works, limits, evidence. Mark anything taken from vendors' own pages or third-party summaries rather than official documentation.

## Caveats

What wasn't checked, what changes quickly (such as prices), and what would change the recommendation.

## Sources

The pages relied on, as links.
